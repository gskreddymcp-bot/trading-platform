# Signal Rules

## Bullish support
- Price above VWAP
- Breadth > 60%
- Fresh put writing below/near spot
- Call unwinding above spot
- Global risk-on context

## Bearish pressure
- Price below VWAP
- Breadth < 40%
- Fresh call writing above/near spot
- Put unwinding below spot
- Global risk-off context

## No trade
- NIFTY and BANKNIFTY conflict
- Breadth neutral
- Option chain contradicts price
- High-impact news risk
- Poor risk-reward
